Frequently asked questions

Did not find your answer? Ask our admin directly on WhatsApp.

Is the bird’s nest already clean?

Yes. Feathers and dirt are lifted out by hand, without chemicals, so all you need to do is soak it and steam it.

Is anything added to it?

No. It is bird’s nest and nothing else: no bleach, no colouring and no additives.

Why is it not pure white?

Natural bird’s nest is ivory to off-white. We do not bleach it, so the colour can differ a little from one harvest to the next.

How do I prepare it?
  1. Soak the nest in enough clean water.
  2. Wait until it has fully expanded.
  3. Pour the soaking water away.
  4. Add fresh, clean water.
  5. Steam for 10 to 15 minutes.

Enjoy it with rock sugar, dates or milk.

How much do I need for one cooking?

About 5 g of dry nest makes two servings. One sachet in our sachet pack is 5 g.

How should I store it?

Keep it in a tightly closed container in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight. Cooked bird’s nest should be kept in the fridge and finished soon.

How much does it cost?

Prices are per pack, in Indonesian rupiah, and are listed on the products page. Please confirm today’s price and stock on WhatsApp before paying.

How long does delivery take?

We ship to addresses across Indonesia, including hotels. Delivery time depends on the city. When you order, our admin confirms the shipping cost, and once the parcel is on its way we send you the tracking number.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer to the company account of PT Lentera Alam Nusantara. We never ask you to transfer to a personal account.

Can I order for an event or for my company?

Yes. We prepare gift boxes and hampers, including seasonal editions for Lunar New Year and Eid, and larger orders for companies. Tell us the quantity, the budget and the delivery date.

Can I take it home on the plane?

It depends on your destination. Dried bird’s nest may not be carried or mailed into mainland China; Singapore allows 1 kg per traveller. See the rules by destination.

Do you ship abroad?

We deliver within Indonesia. Sending bird’s nest across a border depends on the import rules of the destination country. Write to us with the country and the quantity and we will tell you plainly what is possible.

Is Indonesian bird’s nest different from Malaysian bird’s nest?

Both countries produce nests in swiftlet houses, and the island of Borneo, called Kalimantan on the Indonesian side, is shared by both. What you can check matters more than the country name: how the nest was cleaned, whether anything was added, and its shape. Ours comes from Kalimantan and is cleaned by hand without chemicals.

Do you supply importers and wholesalers?

Import rules differ from country to country. Mainland China, for example, only accepts bird’s nest from Indonesian processing plants registered with China Customs (GACC). Tell us your destination and volume, and we will say plainly which forms we can supply and which documents we can provide.
