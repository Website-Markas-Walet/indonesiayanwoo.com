Our process

From swiftlet house to table

Every piece goes through four stages before it reaches you.

  1. HarvestedNests are collected from the swiftlet houses of partner farmers in Kalimantan.
  2. SortedGraded by shape and size: bowl, corner, strips and fine pieces.
  3. CleanedFeathers and dirt are lifted out one by one, by hand, with no bleach or chemicals.
  4. PackedQuality-checked, weighed and packed neatly.

Serving

Soak, steam, enjoy

The nests are already clean, so cooking is simple. Soak in clean water until fully expanded, pour the soaking water away, add fresh water and steam for 10 to 15 minutes. Serve with rock sugar, dates or milk.

About 5 g of dry nest makes two servings.

Who we are

Part of Markaswalet

Markaswalet is a platform owned by PT Lentera Alam Nusantara. It helps swiftlet farmers look after their swiftlet houses, monitor their bird populations and sell their nests at fairer prices. Indonesia Yanwoo brings those nests, packed under our Nestsensials brand, to buyers and visitors from abroad.

About us

Wholesale and import enquiries

Import rules differ from country to country. Mainland China, for example, only accepts bird’s nest from Indonesian processing plants registered with China Customs (GACC). Tell us your destination and volume, and we will say plainly which forms we can supply and which documents we can provide.
