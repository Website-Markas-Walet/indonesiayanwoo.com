Process and quality

Every piece of nest goes through four stages before it reaches you. Nothing is added along the way.

Four stages

  1. HarvestedNests are collected from the swiftlet houses of partner farmers in Kalimantan.
  2. SortedGraded by shape and size: bowl, corner, strips and fine pieces.
  3. CleanedFeathers and dirt are lifted out one by one, by hand, with no bleach or chemicals.
  4. PackedQuality-checked, weighed and packed neatly.

Our cleaning standard

These are the figures we work to when we wash and pack.

  • Below 30 ppmNitrite content. China’s limit for imported bird’s nest is 30 mg/kg.
  • Reverse-osmosis waterNests are washed in RO water with TDS below 40.
  • Ozonated washOzonation is part of the washing step.
  • Heated before packingA heating step before packing, to remove bacteria.

What we do not do

  • No bleach. Natural bird’s nest is ivory to off-white, and we leave it that way.
  • No colouring.
  • No additives of any kind.

Clear origin

We buy directly from partner farmers, without a long chain of middlemen. Every pack comes from a swiftlet house that can be traced through the Markaswalet ecosystem, the platform we use every day to work with swiftlet farmers.
