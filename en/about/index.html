It began in the farmers’ swiftlet houses

Our bird’s nest grew out of Markaswalet, a platform that works with Indonesian swiftlet farmers every day. We want the farmers’ best nests to reach family tables clean and honestly described.

What we stand for

  • Fair to farmersWe buy directly from partner farmers, without a long chain of middlemen.
  • Clean without chemicalsNests are cleaned by hand. No bleach, no colouring, no additives.
  • Clear originEvery pack comes from a swiftlet house that can be traced through the Markaswalet ecosystem.

Part of Markaswalet

Markaswalet is a platform owned by PT Lentera Alam Nusantara. It helps swiftlet farmers look after their swiftlet houses, monitor their bird populations and sell their nests at fairer prices. Selling cleaned nests under the Nestsensials brand is how we bring their harvest straight to the people who eat it.

The name

“Yanwoo” is our spelling of 燕窝 (yànwō), the Chinese word for bird’s nest. This site is where buyers and visitors from abroad can read about our nests in English and Chinese.
