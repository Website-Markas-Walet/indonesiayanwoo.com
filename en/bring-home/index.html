Can you take Indonesian bird’s nest home?

It depends on where home is. Dried bird’s nest may not be carried or mailed into mainland China, while Singapore allows 1 kg per traveller. Below are the rules we checked in November 2026, with the official sources.

DestinationDried bird’s nest in your luggageWhat the rule says
Mainland ChinaNot allowedBird’s nest is on China’s list of animal and plant products that may not be carried or mailed into the country. The only exception is commercially sterilised canned bird’s nest. There is no official personal allowance in grams or kilograms.
SingaporeUp to 1 kg per travellerThe Singapore Food Agency lists cleaned dried bird’s nest from all countries and regions, with a limit of 1 kg per traveller, strictly for personal consumption.
Hong KongConfirm firstWe found no published limit specific to bird’s nest. Hong Kong Customs says food carried in personal baggage for personal consumption, in a reasonable quantity, is generally not subject to control. Confirm with the Centre for Food Safety before you travel. Going on from Hong Kong into the mainland, the mainland rule applies.
TaiwanConfirm firstQuantity limits and animal-quarantine requirements apply. Check with Taiwan Customs and the animal and plant quarantine authority before you buy.
AustraliaDeclare itAustralia’s agriculture department treats bird nests as goods of biosecurity concern. You must declare it on arrival; check the import conditions in BICON before you buy.
Other destinationsConfirm firstWe have not checked every country. Follow the latest notice from the customs and quarantine authorities of your destination.

This page is general information checked in November 2026, not legal advice.

Mainland China: why dried bird’s nest cannot go in your suitcase

  • Answering a traveller in the overseas edition of People’s Daily (24 August 2024), Gongbei Customs wrote: “燕窝禁止携带进境。但经商业无菌处理的罐头装燕窝可以携带进境。” In English: bird’s nest may not be carried into the country, but commercially sterilised canned bird’s nest may. (source)
  • In September 2025 the Hong Kong government confirmed to its legislature that bird’s nest, except commercially sterilised canned products, is still on the mainland’s list and cannot be carried or mailed into the mainland. (source)
  • We could not find any official basis for the “so many grams per person” figures that circulate online.
  • This applies however small the amount is. Declare what you carry, and do not hide it.

If you live in mainland China, what you can do

  • Enjoy it while you are in Indonesia. We deliver to hotels and addresses across the country; a sachet pack is enough for a short stay. See our guide for visitors.
  • Know what is allowed. Only commercially sterilised canned bird’s nest may be carried in. The forms on this site are dried bird’s nest, so they do not fall under that exception.
  • Import through the formal channel. China only accepts bird’s nest from Indonesian processing plants registered with China Customs (GACC). If you are an importer, tell us your destination and volume and we will say plainly what we can supply and which documents we can provide.

Leaving Indonesia

Under Indonesia’s quarantine law (Law No. 21 of 2019), animal products leaving the country must be reported to the quarantine officers at the airport or port, and that includes small amounts carried by hand. Ask at the quarantine counter of your departure airport what is needed for your route, and ask us before you buy if you plan to carry bird’s nest out of the country.

Practical points

  • Keep the original packaging and the proof of purchase.
  • Declare what you carry.
  • Rules change. The latest notice from the destination’s customs and quarantine authorities is what counts.
