Buying bird’s nest while you are in Indonesia

Indonesia is the world’s largest producer of edible bird’s nest. If you are travelling or living here, you can buy close to the source and enjoy it before you leave.

Order in four steps

  1. ChoosePick the form and quantity from the product list.
  2. Message our adminSend your order on WhatsApp. The admin checks stock and the shipping cost.
  3. TransferPay to the company account and send the proof of transfer.
  4. DeliveryYour order is packed and shipped, and we share the tracking number with you.

Tell us your travel dates. Delivery time depends on the city. When you order, our admin confirms the shipping cost and whether the parcel can reach your hotel or address before you leave.

Payment goes only to accounts in the name of PT Lentera Alam Nusantara. We never ask you to transfer to a personal account. If in doubt, confirm with our admin first.

What to choose

  • For a short stayThe sachet pack: five sachets of 5 g. One sachet is one cooking, about two servings, and it is easy to carry.
  • As a giftWhole bowls, or a gift box with a tray of nests and a jar of rock sugar.
  • For the familyStrips or nest cake: the same clean nest at a lower price per pack.

Preparing it where you are staying

  1. Soak the nest in enough clean water.
  2. Wait until it has fully expanded.
  3. Pour the soaking water away.
  4. Add fresh, clean water.
  5. Steam for 10 to 15 minutes.
  6. It is ready to enjoy.

Keep dry nest in a closed container in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Once cooked, keep it in the fridge and finish it soon.

Three simple ideas

  • Rock-sugar soup5 g bird’s nest, 400 ml water, rock sugar to taste, 2 pandan leaves. About 30 minutes, two servings.
  • With milk and dates5 g bird’s nest, 300 ml fresh milk, 4 dates, honey to taste. About 25 minutes, two servings.
  • Warm ginger drink5 g bird’s nest, 2 pieces of roasted ginger, palm sugar to taste, 400 ml water. About 30 minutes, two servings.

Before you fly

Rules for carrying bird’s nest across a border differ a lot. Dried bird’s nest may not be carried or mailed into mainland China; only commercially sterilised canned bird’s nest is allowed. Singapore allows 1 kg of cleaned dried bird’s nest per traveller. Read the rules by destination before you buy for the journey home.
